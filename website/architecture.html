Recovery process

On writable open, SeaWorld validates the format and consistency, then discards an incomplete final transaction tail from its begin record. A fixed read-only open does not repair: it reports recovery required. Corrupt checksummed data and invalid transaction sequences fail closed.

This is a plain-language rendering of the checked-in storage format, transaction and invariant documents.

Consistency & boundaries

V3/V4 retain stable committed views for owning MVCC snapshots and fixed readers. SeaWorld has one writer and bounded concurrent readers; it does not claim Serializable isolation or predicate locking. Persistent single-field secondary and unique indexes are part of the V1/V4 design, while compound, text, geospatial and multikey indexes are not.
